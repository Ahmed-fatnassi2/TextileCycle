**Congratulations!** You now have a 100% fully automated cloud pipeline. Even if you close your laptop, GitHub will talk directly to Azure, and the build will run.

Here is your **"Start-of-Day" Guide** for when you turn your laptop back on and want to work on the project.

---

### ☁️ 1. Wake up Azure (The Server)
When you shut down your laptop, your Azure VM keeps running (and draining your $100 credit) unless you stop it. 

**If you stopped the VM yesterday:**
1. Go to the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com/).
2. Go to **Virtual Machines** -> **`textilecycle-vm2`**.
3. Click **Start** at the top. Wait 1 minute for it to boot.

**If the Azure VM is running, skip this.**

---

### 💻 2. Connect to the VM (Terminal)
Open your WSL terminal on your laptop and SSH into the server:
```bash
ssh ahmed@68.210.230.98
```
*(Enter your password).*

---

### 🔄 3. Restart the Port-Forwards (So you can see SonarQube in your browser)
When you reboot the Azure VM, the internal network tunnels die. You need to restart them so SonarQube works in your browser.

Run this in your **Azure VM terminal**:
```bash
kubectl port-forward -n sonarqube svc/sonarqube-sonarqube 9000:9000 > /tmp/sonar.log 2>&1 &
```
*(This runs it in the background).*

---

### 🌐 4. Access SonarQube (Optional)
If you need to look at SonarQube, open a **new local WSL terminal** (keep the Azure one open) and run:
```bash
ssh -L 9001:localhost:9000 ahmed@68.210.230.98
```
Then open `http://localhost:9001` in your browser.

---

### 🚀 5. Do your DevOps Work!
That's it. You are now fully connected to the cloud. 

*   **To push code:** Just `git push` from your laptop. GitHub will trigger Azure Jenkins automatically (no Ngrok needed). 
*   **To view Jenkins:** `http://68.210.230.98:8080`
*   **To view your App:** `http://68.210.230.98:30080`
*   **To view K8s Pods:** Run `kubectl get pods -n textilecycle` in the Azure terminal.

---

### 🛑 6. IMPORTANT: Shutting Down for the Day
When you are done working and want to close your laptop, **you must stop the VM in Azure** to save your credits.

1. Go to the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com/).
2. Go to **Virtual Machines** -> **`textilecycle-vm2`**.
3. Click **Stop** at the top.
4. **Wait until the status says `Stopped (Deallocated)`.** 
*(If you just close your laptop without doing this, Azure will drain your $100 credit).*
